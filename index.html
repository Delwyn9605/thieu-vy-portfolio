About me

An introvert who loves working with people?

That's me! My name is Phạm Nguyễn Thiệu Vy.

As I grew older, I gradually realized that I felt more fulfilled working with people than with numbers or machines. This led me to make an unexpected choice: going from studying Chemistry at the High School for the Gifted to pursuing Multimedia Communications, a field rooted in the social sciences. Looking back, it was my first real turning point.

When I applied for a volunteer club, all I hoped for was the courage to help those around me, without letting doubts like "Can I really help them?" or "Do they even need me?" make me shrink back.

There, I was placed in the External Relations team and entrusted with responsibilities I had never imagined myself taking on. Outside my family and a small circle of close friends, I had rarely experienced that kind of trust. Little by little, I began to believe in myself and what I could do.
